Digital Access Assistance for Navigation

A clearer way to find your way around campus.

Daan helps students find campus buildings and useful places using the campus map and searchable place directory.

Open campus map → Search places · Click a building · Follow the highlighted route
What you can do

Campus information in one place.

Choose a starting place and destination, or click a building on the map to set your destination.

Find campus places

Search the campus place directory for a building or facility name.

Select a destination

Choose where you are starting from, then select your destination from the directory or map.

Learn about each place

See the selected building and follow the highlighted sample path along the mapped walkways.

Getting started

Three simple steps.

1Open the campus mapUse the button above or the Campus map link.
2Choose a placeSearch the directory, select a destination, or click a building on the map.
3Follow the routeChoose where you are starting and going to see a sample path on the map.
Map reference: The place directory is based on labels visible in the provided campus map. Confirm locations with campus staff. In an emergency, follow staff instructions and the official evacuation plan.
